# Detached Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Detached, published by Man-o-war, lets you control audio and video from your
current browser tab in a floating player.

## What Detached handles

When you open Detached, it uses temporary access to the current tab to find
media. It reads the media title, playback state, available artwork, and relevant
player and playlist controls to show and operate the mini player. If it finds
an embedded player, it can read that player's address to offer to open it in
a tab.

## How Detached uses and shares data

Detached processes this information in memory in your browser to provide the
playback controls you request. It does not save a browsing or playback history,
require an account, run analytics, display advertising, sell user data, or send
page data or viewing activity to Man-o-war.

Playback stays with the original website. In Artwork view, your browser may
request artwork from that site or its image provider. If you choose to open an
embedded player directly, your browser navigates to its address. Those services
handle the requests under their own privacy policies.

## Chrome permissions

Detached uses `activeTab` to get temporary access to the current tab when you
invoke the extension. It uses `scripting` to find media and connect the floating
controls to the source player.

Detached's use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy,
including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact
